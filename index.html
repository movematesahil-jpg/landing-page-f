FAQ

Common questions.

What is FTL transportation?

FTL, or Full Truck Load transportation, is used when a shipment requires an entire truck for movement between locations.

Does Movemate provide dedicated fleet solutions?

Yes. Movemate provides dedicated transportation capacity for businesses with recurring movement requirements.

Can I request a truck for an urgent shipment?

Movemate offers spot freight solutions for businesses that need additional or one-time transportation capacity.

Does Movemate provide LTL transportation?

Yes. LTL transportation is available for shipments that do not require an entire truck.

What industries can Movemate support?

Movemate supports business transportation requirements across industries including manufacturing, FMCG, e-commerce, retail, industrial and pharmaceutical businesses.
